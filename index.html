Building coffee through collaboration

Quality is not built by a single farm. It is the result of collaboration between farmer communities, coffee processors, industry stakeholders, and coffee associations that share a common vision: producing Indonesian Fine Robusta capable of competing in the global market.

Community & Association Support

Through continuous engagement with coffee networks and coffee associations in Banten and Indonesia, farmers gain access to knowledge, quality management practices, post-harvest improvements, and market insights.

Supply Chain Designed for Export Readiness

This collaborative ecosystem supports a reliable supply chain designed to meet the expectations of international buyers while preserving the unique character of Temanggung's volcanic-grown coffee.

From local commodity to global opportunity

For many years, coffee was viewed as a secondary crop by local farming communities. As global demand for high-quality Robusta increased, farmers began to recognize the value of their origin and the unique characteristics of coffee grown in volcanic soils.

Structured production system

Supported by education, industry collaboration, and shared quality standards, farmer communities gradually developed a more structured production system, allowing Temanggung Fine Robusta to offer both distinctive flavor and dependable consistency for long-term partnerships.
